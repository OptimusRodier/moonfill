How the lines work

One line per field: Field label = value. Checkboxes: true or false. Lists and text: the exact text shown in Salesforce.

{today} = today's date (dd/mm/yyyy). {created} = the date on the Created. line of Implementation History. Lines starting with # are ignored, and fields without a value are skipped.

Open an Implementation record: the round pen button appears on the page. Drag it anywhere you like, hover over it to open the menu, pick a preset and click Fill.

Reset (click twice) is for testing: it sets the fields listed in the preset back to --None-- / empty. Fields left out of the preset, such as the notes, are never touched. It follows "After filling": review first, or save automatically.
